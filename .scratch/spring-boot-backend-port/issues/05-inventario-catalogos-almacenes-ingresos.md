# 05: Inventory Catalogs, Producto, Multi-Warehouse StockAlmacen, MovimientoStock & Ingreso

**What to build:** End-to-end inventory classification catalogs (`Proveedor`, `Categoria`, `Familia`, `Marca`), `Producto` catalog with correlative SKU generation and per-warehouse `StockAlmacen` breakdown (`Disponible`, `No disponible`, `Dañado`, `Vencido`), manual stock adjustments, warehouse state transitions and partial transfers, immutable `MovimientoStock` kardex history, and `Ingreso` merchandise purchase receipts (including annulment). `Administrador` manages `Proveedor` (`/api/proveedores`), `Categoria` (`/api/categorias`), `Familia` (`/api/familias`), and `Marca` (`/api/marcas`), while `Recepcionista` can list them. `GET /api/productos` is publicly readable for the store catalog and exposes `stock_por_almacen` and classification names; `GET /api/productos/next-sku` generates the next `2026XXXX` SKU; and `Administrador` can create, update, and delete `Producto` records (`POST/PUT/DELETE /api/productos`). `Administrador` and `Recepcionista` can adjust stock (`PUT /api/productos/{id}/stock`), change warehouse state (`PUT /api/productos/{id}/estado-almacen`), transfer quantities between warehouses (`POST /api/almacenes/mover`), inspect the kardex (`GET /api/movimientos-stock`), and record merchandise purchases (`GET/POST /api/ingresos`), while `Administrador` can delete/annul an `Ingreso` (`DELETE /api/ingresos/{id}`) with stock reversal.

**Blocked by:** 01: Security, JWT Authentication, Usuario CRUD, Password Reset & Auditoria

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] CRUD endpoints for `/api/proveedores`, `/api/categorias`, `/api/familias`, and `/api/marcas` allow `Administrador` and `Recepcionista` to list and `Administrador` to create, update, and delete records, returning `HTTP 409` on duplicate `Categoria`, `Familia`, or `Marca` names.
- [ ] `GET /api/productos/next-sku` returns `{ "sku": "<next>" }` starting from `20260001` for `Administrador` and `Recepcionista`.
- [ ] `GET /api/productos` (publicly accessible) and `POST/PUT/DELETE /api/productos` (`Administrador` only) manage products, initializing `StockAlmacen` on creation, rejecting duplicate SKUs (`HTTP 409`), adjusting `Disponible` stock and logging an `AJUSTE` `MovimientoStock` when `stock` changes on `PUT /api/productos/{id}`, and serializing `proveedor_nombre`, `categoria_nombre`, `familia_nombre`, `marca_nombre`, `estado_almacen`, and `stock_por_almacen`.
- [ ] `PUT /api/productos/{id}/stock` (`sumar` or `restar`), `PUT /api/productos/{id}/estado-almacen`, and `POST /api/almacenes/mover` validate warehouse states and available quantities (`HTTP 400` on insufficient stock or invalid/identical states), update `StockAlmacen` and `Producto`, and record `AJUSTE` or `CAMBIO_ALMACEN` entries in `MovimientoStock`.
- [ ] `GET /api/movimientos-stock` returns kardex entries ordered by `fecha` descending with `producto_nombre` and `usuario_nombre` for `Administrador` and `Recepcionista`.
- [ ] `GET /api/ingresos` and `POST /api/ingresos` validate supplier existence, positive quantities, and non-past `fecha_vencimiento`, increment `Producto.stock` and `StockAlmacen("Disponible")`, update `precio_compra`, `precio`, `es_perecible`, and earliest `fecha_vencimiento`, compute `numero_factura` and `total`, and record `INGRESO` `MovimientoStock` entries; `DELETE /api/ingresos/{id}` (`Administrador` only) reverts `Disponible` stock (clamped at `0`) and records `ANULACION_INGRESO` `MovimientoStock` entries.
- [ ] End-to-end `MockMvc` tests verify catalog CRUD, SKU generation, product CRUD, warehouse transfers, stock adjustments, purchase receipts, purchase annulments, and kardex logging.
