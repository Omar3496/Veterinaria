# 06: POS & Online Venta, ComprobanteSeq Billing, Cancellation, Devolucion & NotaCredito

**What to build:** End-to-end point-of-sale and online store purchasing (`Venta` and `DetalleVenta`), fiscal document validation and correlative receipt numbering (`Boleta` `BA64` and `Factura` `FA64` via `ComprobanteSeq`), `NotaCredito` redemption, pickup code generation (`SRB-XXXXXX`), sale lifecycle state transitions (`Pendiente`, `Confirmada`, `Entregada`, `Anulación solicitada`, `Cancelada`) with stock reversal on cancellation, and post-delivery returns (`Devolucion`) settling via cash reversal (`EXTORNO`) or 180-day store credit (`NotaCredito` `NC-XXXXXX`).

**Blocked by:** 02: Cliente & Mascota Management and Portal Profile, 05: Inventory Catalogs, Producto, Multi-Warehouse StockAlmacen, MovimientoStock & Ingreso

**Status:** resolved

- [x] `POST /api/ventas` allows `Administrador`, `Recepcionista`, and `Cliente` (automatically binding `cliente_id` to the caller's `Cliente` profile when role is `Cliente`) to register a sale atomically (`@Transactional`).
- [x] `POST /api/ventas` validates `tipo_comprobante` (`Boleta` or `Factura`) and `documento_fiscal` (`8`-digit DNI or `12`-digit CE starting with `20` for `Boleta`; `11`-digit RUC for `Factura`), validates that every `Producto` exists, is not expired, is in `Disponible` state, and has sufficient `Producto.stock` and `StockAlmacen("Disponible")`, and rolls back completely if any item fails validation.
- [x] When `nota_credito_id` is supplied on `POST /api/ventas`, the service validates that the `NotaCredito` exists, is `"ACTIVA"`, not expired, belongs to the same `Cliente`, and has `saldo > 0`, deducts `monto_nota = min(total, saldo)` from `total` and `saldo`, and marks the credit note `"CANJEADA"` when `saldo` reaches `0`.
- [x] `POST /api/ventas` deducts `Producto.stock` and `StockAlmacen("Disponible")`, generates a unique `SRB-XXXXXX` `codigo_recojo` and correlative `BA64`/`FA64` receipt number (`010d`), creates `DetalleVenta` records, and logs `VENTA` `MovimientoStock` entries.
- [x] `GET /api/ventas`, `GET /api/ventas/mias`, and `GET /api/ventas/{id}` return sales with `comprobante`, `cliente_nombre`, `vendedor_nombre`, and `detalles` (with `producto_nombre`), enforcing ownership when the caller is a `Cliente`.
- [x] `POST /api/ventas/{id}/solicitar-anulacion` allows a `Cliente` to transition their own `Pendiente` or `Confirmada` sale to `Anulación solicitada`; `PUT /api/ventas/{id}/estado` allows `Administrador` and `Recepcionista` to execute valid transitions (`Pendiente → Confirmada | Cancelada`, `Confirmada → Entregada | Cancelada`, `Anulación solicitada → Cancelada | Confirmada`), restoring stock to `Disponible` and logging `ANULACION_VENTA` `MovimientoStock` entries when transitioning to `Cancelada`.
- [x] `POST /api/ventas/{id}/devolver` allows `Administrador` and `Recepcionista` to process a `Devolucion` (`EXTORNO` or `NOTA_CREDITO`) on an `Entregada` `Venta`, restoring stock to `Disponible`, logging `DEVOLUCION` `MovimientoStock` entries, and issuing a 180-day `NC-XXXXXX` `NotaCredito` when `tipo` is `"NOTA_CREDITO"`; `GET /api/notas-credito` and `GET /api/devoluciones` list credit notes (with computed `vigente` and `cliente_nombre`, filtered to own records for `Cliente`) and returns.
- [x] End-to-end `MockMvc` tests verify sale creation, fiscal and stock/expiry validations, transactional rollback, cancellation requests, state transitions with stock reversal, returns, and credit note issuance and redemption.
